Green umbrella handed in at Florianska 12

A green umbrella with a wooden handle was handed in on 3 October 2026 at the Cafe Nero, Florianska 12, Krakow. The finder, Anna Kowalska, said a man in a grey coat had left it on the terrace around 21:30 and walked towards the Main Square. Anyone who recognises it can call the cafe on +48 12 345 67 89 or write to lost@example.com.

Items are kept for 30 days and then handed over to the city's lost property office on Wielicka 28a. Proof of ownership, such as a photo of the umbrella or a description of its handle, is required to collect it.
